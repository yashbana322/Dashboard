1. OVERVIEW: WHAT WE BUILT
================================================================================
LohanLogistics is an end-to-end, full-stack Transport Brokerage Management 
System designed specifically for Indian road logistics, fleet brokers, commission
agents, and transport contractors.

It completely replaces physical diary entries, paper bilty books, chaotic WhatsApp
records, and cumbersome Excel spreadsheets with a single, synchronized platform.

Key Capabilities:
- Live profit margin locking on every load before truck dispatch.
- Prevention of lost revenue through a dedicated "Manual Leftovers" system for
  halting charges, detention, and short deductions.
- 1-Click Khata Statements with real running debit/credit ledgers that can be
  printed on official LohanLogistics letterheads or shared instantly to WhatsApp.
- Official Digital Bilty (LR) generation supporting Consignor, Consignee, and
  Driver copies with automatic tax and route filling.
- Full Indian GSTIN and PAN compliance with live state detection (Codes 01 to 36)
  and GTA RCM (Reverse Charge Mechanism) support for unregistered truck owners.
- Multi-device connectivity allowing access across other PCs, laptops, and phones
  on the same office Wi-Fi, or over the web.

5. MODULE 3: TRIPS & FLEET DISPATCH (EXPENSES & TRACKING)
================================================================================
Route: /trips
Purpose: Live tracking of long-haul round-trips (Up-load and Down-load), driver
advances, fuel expenses, and net profit closure.

HOW TO USE:
1. TRIP STRUCTURE:
   - Up Journey: Origin to Destination with Client Freight (e.g., Pune to Delhi, Rs. 50,000).
   - Down Journey: Return load to avoid empty running (e.g., Delhi to Pune, Rs. 40,000).
   - Driver Advances: Advance diesel cash and driver bhatta paid upon departure.

2. TRACKING STATUSES:
   - Up Transit: Truck moving towards first destination.
   - Unloading Up: Truck arrived at first consignee depot.
   - Down Transit: Return load picked up and moving back.
   - Delivered / Closed: Load successfully delivered and audited.

3. CLOSING A TRIP & PROFIT AUDIT:
   - When a trip finishes, click [Close Trip].
   - Enter actual fuel consumed, toll receipts, driver wages, and maintenance.
   - System calculates: Total Revenue (Up + Down) minus Total Expenses = Net Cash Profit.

11. MODULE 9: MANUAL LEFTOVERS & DISPUTE CHARGES (+ ADD LEFTOVER)
================================================================================
Shortcut: [+ Add Leftover Due] in the top navigation bar or client drawer.
Purpose: Transport's biggest profit leak is forgotten deductions (unloading 
halting, demurrage, short deductions). This tool ensures you never lose a rupee.

HOW TO USE:
1. WHEN TO USE THIS:
   - Case A: A client holds back Rs. 3,000 for "late delivery" or "short weight".
   - Case B: The driver was halted for 2 days at destination warehouse, and you 
     need to bill Rs. 5,000 vehicle halting charges to the client.
   - Case C: Transporter claims Rs. 1,500 extra toll due to highway route diversion.

2. STEP-BY-STEP ENTRY:
   - Click [+ Add Leftover Due] from the top bar.
   - Party Type: Choose "Client (Receivable)" or "Transporter (Payable)".
   - Select Party Name from the dropdown.
   - Enter Leftover Amount (Rs.): E.g., 4,500.
   - Select Reason Category:
     * Vehicle Halting / Demurrage
     * Short Payment / Weight Shortage Deduction
     * Toll & Route Detour
     * Past Unsettled Trip Balance
     * Other Operational Adjustment
   - Link to Booking: (Optional) Attach to a booking ID (e.g. #1042).
   - Enter Notes: Explain the reason (e.g. "2 days unloading delay at Delhi depot").
   - Click [Record Leftover Balance].
   - Instant effect: The party's balance increases, an audit entry is created, 
     and it is locked into their Khata ledger.

16. MODULE 14: INDIAN GST & PAN TAX COMPLIANCE SYSTEM
================================================================================
Transport brokerage has strict tax compliance rules in India. The app handles 
this automatically across all screens:

KEY COMPLIANCE RULES BUILT IN:
1. 15-CHARACTER INDIAN GSTIN VALIDATION:
   - Enforces format: ^[0-9]{2}[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}[1-9A-Z]{1}Z[0-9A-Z]{1}$
2. AUTOMATIC STATE DETECTION:
   - First 2 digits map to all 36 Indian states and union territories.
   - Example: Typing "27..." displays "📍 Maharashtra (Code 27)".
   - Example: Typing "07..." displays "📍 Delhi (Code 07)".
   - Example: Typing "06..." displays "📍 Haryana (Code 06)".
3. AUTOMATIC PAN EXTRACTION:
   - Characters 3 to 12 of any GSTIN are strictly the entity's 10-digit PAN.
   - The app extracts this automatically—you never have to type PAN twice!
4. GTA REVERSE CHARGE MECHANISM (RCM):
   - Under Indian GST law, individual truck owners are frequently unregistered,
     and tax is paid by the consignor/consignee under RCM.
   - The app includes an "Unregistered under GST (GTA RCM)" option for transporters.

You can use LohanLogistics across multiple computers and phones using 3 methods:

METHOD 1: INSTANT ACCESS ON SAME WI-FI / OFFICE NETWORK (Free, 2 Minutes)
- Both the frontend and backend are configured to listen on all interfaces (0.0.0.0).
- Step 1: On this host PC, your local network IP is: 192.168.31.248
- Step 2: On your second PC, laptop, or phone (connected to the same Wi-Fi):
  Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari) and type:
  http://192.168.31.248:5173
- That's it! Any booking, payment, or bilty created on the other PC immediately 
  saves to this main PC's database.

METHOD 2: ACCESS OVER THE INTERNET FOR FREE (CLOUDFLARE QUICK TUNNEL)
- If you are traveling or working from home:
- Step 1: Download the free tool "cloudflared" on your host PC.
- Step 2: Open PowerShell and run:
  cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:5173
- Step 3: Cloudflare gives you a free HTTPS link (e.g. https://xxxx.trycloudflare.com).
  You can open this link on ANY computer or smartphone in the world!

METHOD 3: 24/7 PERMANENT CLOUD DEPLOYMENT (RENDER + VERCEL)
- If you want the app running online 24/7 without keeping your PC powered on:
- Backend: Deploy the "backend" folder to Render.com or Railway.app (Free Node service).
- Frontend: Deploy the "frontend" folder to Vercel.com (Free React hosting).
- Set Environment Variable in Vercel:
  VITE_API_BASE = https://your-backend.onrender.com/api
- You receive a permanent .vercel.app link accessible anywhere.

19. DAY-IN-THE-LIFE: STEP-BY-STEP BROKER WORKFLOW EXAMPLE
================================================================================
Here is how an Indian transport broker uses this app throughout a normal business day:

09:00 AM - MORNING AUDIT:
- Open Dashboard (/). Check Active Trips on highway transit and review pending 
  Receivables from clients.

09:30 AM - RECORDING A NEW LOAD:
- Client "Tata Steel Wire Div" calls needing a 32 FT Container from Pune to Delhi.
- Go to Bookings -> [+ Create Booking].
- Select "Tata Steel Wire Div". Route: Pune -> Delhi. Vehicle: 32 FT Container.
- Enter Client Rate: Rs. 50,000. Transporter Rate: Rs. 41,000.
- System confirms expected margin: Rs. 9,000. Click Confirm.

10:30 AM - DISPATCH & BILTY GENERATION:
- Truck arrives at loading dock (MH 12 AB 1234).
- Click [Digital Bilty] from top bar. Select the Tata Steel booking.
- Consignor GSTIN and address auto-fill. Enter Consignee details and driver license.
- Click [Print Bilty (LR)] for the physical copy.
- Click [Share WhatsApp] to send the LR copy to the driver's phone.

02:00 PM - RECORDING A VEHICLE HALTING DEDUCTION:
- Driver calls from Delhi: Factory warehouse is crowded, unloading delayed by 24 hours.
- Broker calls Tata Steel: Agreed on Rs. 3,000 halting charges.
- Click [+ Add Leftover Due] in top bar.
- Party: Tata Steel Wire Div. Amount: Rs. 3,000. Reason: Vehicle Halting.
- Click [Record Leftover]. The client's ledger automatically updates to reflect 
  Rs. 53,000 total due.

05:00 PM - LOGGING ADVANCE RECOVERY:
- Tata Steel accounts team transfers Rs. 30,000 advance via NEFT.
- Go to Receivables -> [Record Payment].
- Client: Tata Steel Wire Div. Amount: Rs. 30,000. Mode: NEFT. Enter Bank UTR.
- Click [Save Payment Receipt]. Remaining leftover due becomes Rs. 23,000.

06:00 PM - SENDING MONTH-END KHATA STATEMENT:
- Click [Party Khata] in top bar. Select "Tata Steel Wire Div".
- Full ledger displays: Trip Freight (Rs. 50,000) + Halting (Rs. 3,000) - NEFT (Rs. 30,000) = Rs. 23,000 Leftover Balance.
- Click [Share WhatsApp] -> [Send to WhatsApp] to send the formal statement 
  directly to Tata Steel's dispatch manager.

20. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================
Q1: Where is my data stored?
A: In backend/data/db.json on your computer. It is saved in plain JSON format 
   and automatically backed up every time you make an edit.

Q2: What happens if I accidentally delete a client or trip?
A: Open [Data Management] -> [Backup & Restore] to download backups regularly. 
   You can restore any previous backup in 1 second.

Q3: Can I run this offline without an active internet connection?
A: Yes! The entire app runs locally on your PC. You only need an internet connection
   if you want to share via WhatsApp Web or access it from outside the office.

Q4: How do I restart the servers if my PC restarts?
A: Open two PowerShell windows:
   Window 1 (Backend):
     cd e:\Son\transport-broker\backend
     npm run dev
   Window 2 (Frontend):
     cd e:\Son\transport-broker\frontend
     npm run dev
   Then open http://localhost:5173 in your browser.
